# Complete exploratory research artifacts

`rl-study-001-full.zip` contains all ten runs from the first paired five-seed DQN/tabular study: successful and unsuccessful learned policies, full learning curves, protocols, exact training-source snapshots, and a per-file SHA-256 manifest. All patients and outcomes are synthetic.

`rl-study-002-full.zip` contains all 15 runs from the paired five-seed assisted V1/V2 tabular comparison and unassisted V2 ablation. It preserves every checkpoint, complete learning curves and raw comparator episodes, exact frozen sources, protocols, summary, configurations, logs, and a per-file SHA-256 manifest. Its [study report](../docs/rl-study-002.md) includes all zero-success and harmful runs, paired return intervals, and validation-selection bias. The assisted V2-minus-V1 return interval includes zero and does not establish superiority.

`rl-study-003-full.zip` contains all ten runs from the fresh paired five-seed assisted V2/V3 representation and numerical-convention comparison. It retains all 30,000 learning-curve records, including negative returns, every checkpoint and raw comparator episode, frozen sources, protocol, audited summary, configurations, CSVs, and logs. The [study report](../docs/rl-study-003.md) shows V3's one observed severe injury, zero-success runs, and a paired return interval spanning zero; the study does not establish improvement. Its two validation-selected candidates remain separate from the live reference.

`rl-engineering-development-full.zip` preserves all ten saved early engineering runs, including harmful and zero-success outcomes. Its manifest maps the 13 original JSON/protocol files to their `.data` paths, hashes, and outcome summaries. Some records use older model versions and lack complete exact-source provenance; they are development evidence and must not be pooled with the frozen studies as matched experiments. The accompanying checksum and [archive script](../scripts/archive_engineering_runs.py) support integrity checks and reproducible packaging without new simulation.

[`rl-final-evaluation-001-full.zip`](rl-final-evaluation-001-full.zip) preserves the single authorized final comparison: 12 frozen policies and three baselines on 128 shared synthetic patients, totaling 1,920 recorded rollouts. It includes every raw result and JSONL row, all copied policy inputs, prespecified design/freeze/analysis plans, exact inference/physics/evaluator sources, reporter and locked requirements, the 60,565-seed exclusion evidence, historical review and later explicit authorization, independent numerical audit, execution log, and complete report tables and figures. These are 128 shared patients, not 1,920 independent patients; the archived report distinguishes fixed-policy intervals from the predeclared crossed analysis. No policy is selected or promoted by packaging these records.

Final-archive entries retain repository-relative paths, including the hidden `.data` directory. Every payload is covered by `MANIFEST-SHA256.json`; fixed ZIP metadata, byte-identical double builds, CRCs, and all payload hashes were checked. The archived `scripts/independent-audit-source.py` is the exact evidence-generation script with its original absolute workspace paths, not a portable installed command. The included [reporter](../scripts/report_frozen_evaluation.py) supports public report reproduction with explicit input/output paths.

Verify the archive with the accompanying `.sha256` file before extraction. The archive is a research record, not the Windows application installer. Checkpoints are declarative JSON; none is a clinically validated controller. The study's weak average performance, harmful runs, and validation-selection bias are described in [the study report](../docs/rl-study-001.md).

The application's bundled reference is a single explicitly validation-selected example. The complete archives preserve the other results so that selecting an example does not conceal failures. Study 002 and 003 candidates remain separate from the application's default. None of the three frozen development/validation studies includes the reserved final-test cohort.
